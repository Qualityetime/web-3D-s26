Immersive Design

VR Environment

This project is an immersive VR world built in Unreal Engine. I focused on atmosphere, lighting, scale, navigation, and environmental storytelling so the space feels like something the viewer can actually step into and explore.

Watch Trailer → ← Back to Work

Project Goal

The goal was to create a VR environment that felt atmospheric and intentional instead of just being a simple 3D scene. I wanted the viewer to feel scale, mood, and movement through the way the world was arranged.

Tools Used

  • Unreal Engine
  • Meta Quest VR
  • 3D environmental assets
  • Lighting and material setup

What I Built

I built the scene layout, adjusted the lighting, organized the environment, tested VR navigation, and created a trailer to document the final walkthrough experience.
