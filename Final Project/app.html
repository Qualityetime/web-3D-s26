Bus App Redesign

This bus app redesign was a collaborative UI/UX project created in Figma with two other team members. The goal was to improve the look, structure, and usability of an existing transportation app by making the interface feel clearer, more organized, and easier to navigate. Our redesign focused on improving the user experience for students and riders who need quick access to routes, arrival times, stops, and trip information.

Project Goal

The goal of this project was to take a bus app that felt confusing or outdated and redesign it into something more readable and user-friendly. We focused on making important information easier to find, especially route details, bus arrival times, and navigation options. Since transportation apps are used quickly and often under time pressure, we wanted the design to feel simple, direct, and practical.

Tools Used

  • Figma
  • UI layout design
  • Wireframing and prototyping
  • Collaborative design planning
  • User experience research and feedback

What I Built

For this project, I worked with two other team members to plan and redesign the app interface in Figma. I contributed to the layout, visual direction, screen organization, and overall user flow. My role involved helping decide how information should be arranged on the screen so the app would feel easier to use. I also helped refine the visual style so the redesign felt cleaner, more modern, and more consistent across the different screens.
